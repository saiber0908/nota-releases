Materials

Slides in Serbian make sense from the first page

Add a PDF, PowerPoint, Word file or a photo of the board. Nota writes a summary in your native language and a glossary, and underlines the terms right on the slide: hover to see the translation and an explanation.

  • A summary with the key ideas, formulas and likely test questions
  • A translation of any page next to the original
  • Send terms to your “From school” flashcards in one click
  • Ask the tutor about the page you have open

Tutor

Explains instead of doing it for you

The tutor knows your materials and uses the same notation as your teacher. It draws formulas, graphs, diagrams, tables and chemical equations right in the answer and reveals solutions step by step, so you can try first.

  • Four modes: explain a topic, guiding questions, check a solution from a photo, show the solution
  • Save any part of an answer to your notes with one button
  • Remembers what you find hard and shows exactly what it sees from your materials
  • Voice input, with speech recognized right on your computer

Circle to ask

Press Ctrl+Shift+X in any app and circle a formula, a problem or a chart. A question to the tutor opens with your selection attached — just say what is unclear.

Why is it 2a in the denominator and not just a?

Tests

Prepare for a test with a plan, not the night before

Set the date and pick your materials. Nota finds the topics, checks what you already know and spreads the work across the days. The day before, take a timed mock test, just like the real one.

  • Readiness for every topic, based on your answers rather than a guess
  • Seven question types, from multiple choice to formula problems and written answers
  • Go over your mistakes with the tutor in one click
  • A knowledge map for each subject: what is clear and what needs a refresh

Languages

Serbian, English and Spanish, up to C1

A placement test, then a path of 38 lessons: words, a grammar rule, exercises, listening and a role-play. Between lessons, flashcards, conversations and writing with corrections.

  • Flashcards with pictures

    FSRS spaced repetition. Concrete words get a photo from free image libraries.

  • “From school” deck

    Terms from your own slides become flashcards with an example from the slide.

  • Conversations and writing

    Role-plays at the bakery, the doctor’s or school. The AI corrects your text and explains why.

  • Pronunciation

    Say a word out loud and Nota marks what sounded unclear. Speech is recognized on your computer.

For Serbian: Latin and Cyrillic everywhere, case tables and false friends.

On your desktop

Learn words in between

The widget shows a new word every few minutes: click for the translation and an example, and “Know it” or “Again” goes straight into your review schedule. Ctrl+Shift+Space opens a quick question to the tutor from any app.

  • A widget in three sizes
  • Windows notifications with the word and buttons
  • Tray icon and start with Windows, if you want

Presentations

A talk from the outline to the rehearsal

Claude suggests an outline, then makes the slides and the speaker notes. Edit right on the slide, rehearse with a timer and a voice recording, and get feedback afterwards: where it drags and which filler words you use.

  • Themes, charts, formulas and photos with author credit

  • A check against your teacher’s requirements and a bibliography in APA, MLA or GOST

Setup

Runs on your Claude subscription

Nota does not sell AI access or route your requests through its own server. It runs the official Claude Code on your computer, and you sign in to it in Anthropic’s window with your own subscription.

  1. 1

    Install Nota

    A small installer that needs no admin rights. Updates arrive on their own.

  2. 2

    Connect Claude

    Nota finds Claude Code or installs it with Anthropic’s official installer. A Pro or Max plan works.

  3. 3

No subscription? Your own Anthropic API key works too. It is kept in Windows’ secure credential store.

FAQ

The short version

How much does Nota cost?

Nota is free right now. The AI is paid separately — through a Claude Pro or Max plan or your own Anthropic API key.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11. There is no Mac, web or phone version yet.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say “Windows protected your PC”. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Nota updates are signed, and Nota checks the signature before installing them.

What language is the app in?

The interface is in Russian for now; English and Serbian are planned. The tutor explains in the native language you choose: English, Serbian, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Russian, Ukrainian or Belarusian.

Is this an Anthropic app?

No. Nota is an independent project. It runs on Claude Code, Anthropic’s official tool, but is not affiliated with Anthropic.

What goes over the internet?

Your questions and the materials they need go to Claude through your own Claude Code or key. Picture search for words uses Wikimedia Commons and Openverse, and Nota checks GitHub for updates. Nothing else.

How do updates work?

Nota finds a new version and downloads it in the background. Just restart — your materials, chats and flashcards stay where they are.
